01  The studio

Skin first,colour second.

Muse is a small makeup studio built around prep, light and restraint. We match tone and texture to your skin, then build colour to suit the room, the camera and the hours ahead.

Every booking starts with a short consultation so the look is agreed before the first brush touches your face.
